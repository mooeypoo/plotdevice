# Story beats

## Act one

- Opening image
- The theme is spoken
- The ordinary world
- The moment everything changes
- The hero hesitates

## Act two

- Into a new world
- A second story begins
- The promise of the premise
- Midpoint: a false victory or a false defeat
- Pressure closes in
- All is lost
- The darkest moment

## Act three

- A new plan
- The finale
- Closing image
