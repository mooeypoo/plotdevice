# Writer sessions: a guide for running them

For the owner, to run short sessions with two or three writers before the next big features (Milestone 20). The tasks come from `UX_SPEC.md` ("UX validation tasks with authors"), plus backups and the sample book. Each session takes about 45 minutes and is held in the writer's own language, Hebrew or English.

## Before the session

- **Install the latest build** on the writer's computer, or on a laptop you bring. Their own computer is better: it shows the real installing and folders.
- **Don't open the app first.** The start screen is part of the test.
- **Have a small Word document ready** with two or three pages of their writing, or a sample, for "Open Markdown in another app" and for talking about importing.
- **Ask permission** to take notes, and to record the screen if they agree. Say that you're testing the app, not them, and that there are no wrong answers.

## How to run it

- **Give each task in one sentence,** then stay quiet. Don't point, don't explain the screen, and don't rescue them unless they're stuck for about two minutes.
- **Ask them to think aloud:** "Tell me what you're looking for."
- **Write down for each task:** whether they finished, where they hesitated (and for how long, roughly), what they said, and every wrong turn.
- **After each task,** ask: "How clear was that, from 1 (lost) to 5 (obvious)?"

## The tasks

1. **Start a book.** "You're starting a new novel. Set it up." (The template picker; where it's kept; the language.)
2. **Try the sample.** "Before writing, have a look around a finished example." (Try a sample book; then Start the sample over.)
3. **Write without planning.** "Write the first paragraph of a chapter." (Write; saving; formatting.)
4. **Write mixed-direction dialogue.** Hebrew sessions only: "Write a line where someone says an English name or a number."
5. **Find a character detail without losing your place.** "While writing, check how Bea is related to Ilka." (The shelf.)
6. **Move an idea on the board.** "Move this scene to the middle of the story." (Story, Board, dragging or the Move menu.)
7. **Write an outline without typing a Markdown symbol.** "Plan the next three scenes."
8. **Reorganize chapters.** "Swap the order of chapters 2 and 3."
9. **Start a campaign from a template.** For writers who play tabletop games, or as a variation: "Set up a D&D campaign and add a villain."
10. **Keep the book safe.** "Make sure you won't lose this book if your computer breaks." (Backups…, choosing a synced folder; what they understand about "never deleted".)
11. **Restore.** "You deleted a paragraph yesterday and want it back." (Earlier versions or Restore from a backup: which did they reach for, and why?)
12. **Open the book in another app.** "Open this chapter in Word or another program." (The book's folder; what they think the files are.)
13. **Fix something by hand.** Only for writers comfortable with it: "There's a stray symbol in this chapter; fix it in plain text."

## What to watch for

- **Words they didn't understand,** in the app or the guide. Write the exact word down.
- **Where they expected something to be.** For example, did they look for backups in Settings or in the book's menu?
- **Fear:** hesitating before a button because it might delete or change something. Note which buttons.
- **The "?" links:** did they notice them, and did they help?
- **Hebrew sessions:** anything that reads as translated rather than written in Hebrew; direction problems; gendered wording that felt wrong.

# מפגשים עם כותבים: מדריך להנחיה

הנחיות קצרות לבעלת הפרויקט, למפגשים עם שניים או שלושה כותבים לפני הפיצ'רים הגדולים הבאים. המשימות הן אלה שלמעלה. כל מפגש נמשך כ־45 דקות, בשפה של הכותב או הכותבת.

- **לפני:** להתקין את הגרסה האחרונה, עדיף במחשב של הכותב; לא לפתוח את האפליקציה מראש; להכין מסמך Word קצר; לבקש רשות לרשום, ולהבהיר שבודקים את האפליקציה, לא אותם.
- **במהלך:** לתת כל משימה במשפט אחד ולשתוק; לבקש לחשוב בקול; לא להצביע ולא להציל, אלא אם נתקעים כשתי דקות; לרשום היסוסים, טעויות ומילים שלא הובנו.
- **אחרי כל משימה:** "כמה זה היה ברור, מ־1 (אבוד) עד 5 (מובן מאליו)?"
- **לשים לב במיוחד:** מילים שלא הובנו; איפה חיפשו דברים; חשש לפני כפתורים; האם הבחינו בסימני ה"?"; עברית שנשמעת מתורגמת, בעיות כיוון, ולשון זכר או נקבה שהרגישו לא נכון.
- **אחרי המפגש:** למלא את הטופס שלמעלה, ולפתוח issue לכל ממצא, עם התווית `writer-session`.
