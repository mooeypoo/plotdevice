Decisions to approve

D1

Write is the page; Plot is the whole story

Board, Overview, Timeline and Interactions move into Plot. The Write screen loses its view tabs, so "Write" appears once.

D2

Settings leave the top bar

Interface language, layouts, appearance and Close project move into one ⋯ menu. The welcome screen keeps the language picker.

D3

Board items are beats

"+ Add beat" on the board; "card" means only an entry you pinned.

D4

The outline comes first beside the page

Standard opens with Manuscript on one side and Outline first on the other. Details becomes compact.

D5

Parts and columns are both one click away

The Manuscript side keeps "+ New part" beside "+ New chapter", as today. The board gains "+ Add column" after the last column, which adds one ## heading to the outline; if no part has that name, it offers to create the part too.

D6

"Details", not "Properties"

The panel with an entry's keys is called Details. "Edit details" opens it from a peek, a pinned card, the Chapter card or a Manuscript row.
