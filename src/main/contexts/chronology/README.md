# chronology

**Purpose.** Story time and plotlines: parsing and comparing story dates under the project's calendar, and the threads defined in `novel.md`.

**Main concepts.** `StoryDate` (single dates and ranges), `Calendar` (`gregorian` or `day-count`), `Thread`, `TimelineEntry`.

**Must not know about.** Chapter order, prose, or codex entries. The timeline grid that combines chapters with dates is a read model in `src/main/queries/`.

**Status.** Empty until Phase 7.
