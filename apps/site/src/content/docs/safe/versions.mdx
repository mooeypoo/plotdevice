Plot Device keeps earlier versions of every chapter, outline and page as you work: before it first changes a file, and then at most every ten minutes while you keep editing.

## See them

- **A chapter:** the book menu (the book's title) → **<Ui k="chapter-versions" />**.
- **An outline:** **⋯** beside the views → **<Ui k="outline-versions" />**.
- **A page:** **<Ui k="versions" />** on the page.

Versions are listed by time. Choose one to read it.

## Bring one back

- **<Ui k="versions-restore" />** puts it back. <Ui k="versions-restore-detail" />
- **<Ui k="versions-copy" />** keeps it as a separate file instead, leaving the current text as it is.

## Where they're kept, and for how long

On your computer, outside the book's folder, so your book stays clean and you can copy or sync it freely:

- **Linux:** `~/.config/plotdevice/backups/`
- **Windows:** `%APPDATA%\plotdevice\backups\`

They're tidied as they age:

- every version from the last 24 hours;
- then one a day, for 30 days;
- then one a week, for a year.

A year of history for a 100-chapter book takes about 100 MB.

Earlier versions protect against mistakes, not against losing the computer. For that, turn on [backups](/plotdevice/safe/backups/).
