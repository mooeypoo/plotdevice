---
title: Backups
description: Where Plot Device keeps earlier versions of your files, and for how long.
---

Plot Device keeps a copy of a file, exactly as it was, before it changes it. These copies are what [Earlier versions](/plotdevice/safe/versions/) shows.

## Where

On your computer, outside the book's folder, so your book stays clean and you can copy or sync it freely:

- **Linux:** `~/.config/plotdevice/backups/`
- **Windows:** `%APPDATA%\plotdevice\backups\`

There's one folder per book, named by the book's id (the `id` in its `project.md`).

## How long

- Every version from the last 24 hours.
- Then one a day, for 30 days.
- Then one a week, for a year.

A year of history for a 100-chapter book takes about 100 MB.

## Your own backups

These copies protect against mistakes, not against losing the computer. Keep your book's folder in a backed-up or synced place as well, as you would any important document.
