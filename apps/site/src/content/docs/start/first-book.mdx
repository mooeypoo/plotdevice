You can start writing within a minute. Planning is there when you want it, never before.

## Start a book

1. On the welcome screen, choose **<Ui k="welcome-new-project" />**.
2. Give the book a **<Ui k="new-project-title-label" />**. The **<Ui k="new-project-author-label" />** is optional.
3. Choose the **<Ui k="new-project-language-label" />**. It sets the direction your paragraphs run: Hebrew right to left, English left to right. You can still mix languages inside the book.
4. Choose **<Ui k="new-project-create" />**, then the place on your computer to keep the book's folder.

<Shot name="new-project" alt="The welcome screen with the new book form: title, name on the cover and language" />

## Write

The book opens in **<Ui k="workspace-write" />**. Choose **+ <Ui k="navigator-new-chapter" />** at the side, give the chapter a title, and start typing.

There is no save button. Plot Device saves a moment after you stop typing, and whenever you switch chapters or leave the window. The top bar says **<Ui k="save-saved" />** when everything is on disk.
