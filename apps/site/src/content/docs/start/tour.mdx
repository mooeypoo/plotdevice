## The top bar

- **The book's title** opens the book menu: **<Ui k="export-book" />** and **<Ui k="chapter-versions" />**.
- **<Ui k="workspace-story" /> | <Ui k="workspace-write" />** switches between planning and writing. Use them in any order; your place in each is kept.
- **<Ui k="save-saved" />** tells you everything is on disk.
- **Aa** changes how the page looks: text size, page width and focus mode.
- **☰** has the rest: open another book, **<Ui k="settings-open" />**, help, and About.

<Shot name="book-menu" alt="The book menu, open under the book's title" close />

## Write

- **The chapter list** shows the book in reading order, with its parts. The icon at the top of the page hides and shows it.
- **The page** is where you write. Select text to format it.
- **<Ui k="shelf" />** (the book icon at the top right of the page) keeps characters, places and notes beside the page.
- **¶** has every formatting command, for when nothing is selected or you prefer the keyboard.

## Story

**<Ui k="workspace-story" />** holds your outlines, characters, places and notes. See [Outlines](/plotdevice/planning/outlines/).
