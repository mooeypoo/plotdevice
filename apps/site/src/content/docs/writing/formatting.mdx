Formatting looks like formatting: a heading is a heading, bold is bold, and a link shows as its words. The marks that make them, such as `##` and `**`, are never on the page, even where you're typing.

## Select text

Select some text and a small bubble appears above it: **<Ui k="format-bold" />**, **<Ui k="format-italic" />**, **<Ui k="format-link" />**, **<Ui k="format-quote" />**, and **⋯** for the rest.

## The ¶ menu

**¶**, at the top right of the page, has every command, with or without a selection:

<Shot name="format-menu" alt="The ¶ menu: bold, italic, heading, quote, lists, scene break, link and direction" close />

- **<Ui k="format-heading1" />**, **<Ui k="format-heading2" />** and **<Ui k="format-heading3" />** make the paragraph you're in a heading of that size; **<Ui k="format-body" />** makes it ordinary text again.
- **<Ui k="format-quote" />**, **<Ui k="format-bulletList" />** and **<Ui k="format-orderedList" />** apply to the paragraph you're in.
- **<Ui k="format-sceneBreak" />** inserts a break between scenes. It shows as **⁂** and is written as `* * *` in the file.
- **<Ui k="format-link" />** asks for an address. To link a card to a chapter or a character, see [Linking](/plotdevice/planning/linking/).
- The direction commands are explained in [Hebrew and English in one book](/plotdevice/writing/hebrew-and-english/).

## Keyboard

| Press | To |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl+B** (⌘B on a Mac) | Bold |
| **Ctrl+I** (⌘I) | Italic |
| **Ctrl+Alt+1**, **2**, **3** (⌘⌥1, 2, 3) | Heading 1, 2 or 3, as in Word and Google Docs |
| **Ctrl+Alt+0** (⌘⌥0) | Body text |
| **Backspace**, at the start of a heading or a bullet | Make it ordinary text (a bullet one step in moves out a step) |
| **Enter** | Start a new paragraph |
| **Shift+Enter** | Break the line without a new paragraph |
| **Tab**, from the page | Reach the bubble's buttons; **Escape** returns to the page |

If you know Markdown, typing it works too: `## ` at the start of a line makes a heading, `- ` a bullet and `**word**` bold, and the marks disappear as soon as they're understood.

## Links

A link shows as its words, in the link colour. Edit them like any words: **Backspace** erases a letter at a time, and the link goes only when its last letter does. Typing right after a link is ordinary text, outside it.

With the cursor in a link, a small bubble says where it goes and offers **<Ui k="link-open" />**, **<Ui k="link-change" />** (choose another page, chapter or address; the words stay) and **<Ui k="link-remove" />** (the words stay, as ordinary text).

## Plain text

Underneath, formatting is ordinary Markdown (`**bold**`, `*italic*`, `> quote`), so the same text reads well in any other app. To see and fix the marks by hand, choose **<Ui k="plain-text-edit" />**: in **¶** on a chapter, in **⋯** on a page or an outline. Every mark shows, and what you type is saved exactly as typed.

<Shot name="plain-text" alt="A chapter in plain text: the links and the scene break written out as Markdown, under the plain text notice" />
 **<Ui k="plain-text-back" />** returns, with undo and your place kept.

To always open pages this way, see [Settings](/plotdevice/reference/settings/).
