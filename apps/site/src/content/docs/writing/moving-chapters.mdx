title: Moving chapters
description: Rearrange the book by dragging, with the keyboard, or from a chapter's menu.

Moving a chapter changes the book's reading order, nothing else: no file is renamed, and the text isn't touched.

## Drag

Drag a chapter in the list. A gold line shows where it will land:

- Over the top half of a chapter, it goes **before** it; over the bottom half, **after** it.
- Just below a part's last chapter, it stays **inside** the part. Over the top of the next entry, it goes **outside**.
- Over a part's name: above it is outside the part, below it is the part's first place.

## With the keyboard

Focus a chapter in the list and press **Alt+↑** or **Alt+↓**. It moves one step through the reading order, in and out of parts, and keeps the focus.

## From the menu

A chapter's **⋯** has **<Ui k="chapter-move-up" />**, **<Ui k="chapter-move-down" />**, and **<Ui k="chapter-move-to" />**: the start of the book, the end of any part, or the end of the book.

## Move for later

**<Ui k="chapter-remove" />** takes a chapter out of the reading order and keeps it under **<Ui k="navigator-not-in-book" />**, with its text intact. To bring it back, choose **<Ui k="navigator-add-to-book" />**, or drag it into the book where you want it.

Chapter files added to the book's folder by another app also appear under **<Ui k="navigator-not-in-book" />**, until you add them.
