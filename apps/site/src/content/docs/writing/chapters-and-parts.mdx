A book is a list of **chapters**, in reading order. Chapters can be grouped into **parts**, such as "Part One: Arrival". Parts are optional.

## Add a chapter or a part

- **+ <Ui k="navigator-new-chapter" />** adds a chapter right after the one you're writing, and opens it.
- **+ <Ui k="navigator-new-part" />** adds a part at the end of the book. Drag chapters into it.

Chapter numbers are worked out by Plot Device from the reading order. They are never written into your files, so moving a chapter never renumbers anything by hand.

## A chapter's menu

Point at a chapter and choose **⋯**:

<Shot name="chapter-menu" alt="A chapter's menu: rename, rename the file, move, and move for later" close />

- **<Ui k="chapter-rename" />** changes the chapter's title.
- **<Ui k="chapter-rename-file" />** changes the name of its file. Renaming the title never renames the file by itself.
- **<Ui k="chapter-move-up" />**, **<Ui k="chapter-move-down" />** and **<Ui k="chapter-move-to" />** move it. See [Moving chapters](/plotdevice/writing/moving-chapters/).
- **<Ui k="chapter-remove" />** takes it out of the reading order without deleting anything. It waits under **<Ui k="navigator-not-in-book" />** until you add it back.

A part's **⋯** renames the part.

## Missing chapters

If a chapter's file was moved or deleted outside Plot Device, it appears under **<Ui k="navigator-missing" />**. When Plot Device finds a likely match, it offers to use it. It never deletes or relinks anything by itself.
