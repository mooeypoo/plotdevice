Settings belong to you, not to the book: they're kept on your computer, and opening the book elsewhere doesn't carry them along.

## ☰ → <Ui k="settings-open" />

Or **Ctrl+,** (⌘,).

- **<Ui k="settings-language" />**: English or Hebrew, for menus and buttons. Your book keeps its own language.
- **<Ui k="settings-theme" />**: <Ui k="theme-system" />, <Ui k="theme-light" /> or <Ui k="theme-dark" />.

## Aa, while you write

- **<Ui k="settings-writing-size" />** and **<Ui k="settings-page-width" />**.
- **<Ui k="focus-enter" />**.

See [How the page looks](/plotdevice/writing/the-page/).
