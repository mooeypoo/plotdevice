## Does it need the internet, or an account?

No. Plot Device runs on your computer and sends nothing anywhere.

## Is it open source?

Yes. Its source is open, under the GNU GPL, [on GitHub](https://github.com/mooeypoo/plotdevice).

## Where is my book?

In the folder you chose when you started it. It's ordinary Markdown files, readable in any app. See [The book's folder](/plotdevice/files/folder/).

## Can I use it with Obsidian, Dropbox or git?

Yes. Plot Device notices changes made by other apps and never overwrites them without asking. Avoid typing in the same chapter in two apps at once.

## Does it work on a Mac?

Not yet: early versions are for Linux and Windows. A macOS version will come later.

## Can I write in Hebrew? In both Hebrew and English?

Yes. See [Hebrew and English in one book](/plotdevice/writing/hebrew-and-english/).

## Can I get my book as Word or EPUB?

Export it as one Markdown file, then convert it with Pandoc. See [Exporting the book](/plotdevice/sharing/export/).

## Does Plot Device use AI?

No.

See [Report a problem](/plotdevice/about/problems/).
