**<Ui k="workspace-story" />** has two halves. **<Ui k="story-plot" />** is the story's events: its outlines. **<Ui k="story-world" />** is who and where: **<Ui k="story-character" />**, **<Ui k="story-place" />** and **<Ui k="story-note" />**. Each page is a Markdown file in its own folder.

## See them all

Under **<Ui k="story-world" />**, choose **<Ui k="story-character" />**, **<Ui k="story-place" />** or **<Ui k="story-note" />**. They open as cards, A to Z: each with its name, other names, description, tags and first relationships.

<Shot name="world-gallery" alt="The characters as cards: Bea Halmi, Ilka Varga and Noël Arany" />

## Add a page

The last card, **+ <Ui k="new-character" />** (or **+ <Ui k="new-place" />**, **+ <Ui k="new-note" />**), adds one. Give it a name.

## Write it

Click a card to open the page. <Ui k="reference-hint" />

The description is what the references shelf shows first; the details open under it. Use headings and lists in the details as you like. **‹** above the name goes back to the cards.

## Relationships

Beside the page, **<Ui k="relationships" />** lists how it connects to other pages: "Aunt of Ilka", "Waits at the Ferry Landing".

<Shot name="page-connections" alt="Bea's page, with her relationships and what links to her beside it" />

- **+ <Ui k="relationship-add" />**: pick a page, then say how they're related, such as "Sister of".
- A relationship's **⋯** changes its words or removes it.

A relationship goes one way: Bea is Ilka's aunt. Ilka's page shows it under **<Ui k="linked-from" />**, and her file isn't changed. In the page's text, the relationships are one quiet line, so they aren't shown twice; click it to see them as written. See [Character, place and note pages](/plotdevice/files/pages/) for how they look in the file.

## Tags

Under a page's name, **<Ui k="tag-add" />** adds a tag: your own words for a group, such as "Part Two – main", "Varga family" or "the Marrow plot". Tags you've already used are suggested as you type, so the same tag isn't spelled two ways. **×** takes a tag off this page.

A page can have as many tags as you like, and a tag can be on characters, places and notes alike.

## Group by tags

Above the cards, choose one or more tags. Their pages come first, one group for each tag, in the order you chose them. Everyone else folds away below. Choose a tag again to let it go.

<Shot name="world-tags" alt="The characters grouped: Main first, then Part Two – main, and everyone else folded below" />

The references shelf (while you search it) and **<Ui k="link-to" />** have the same row of tags. While you write, the shelf remembers the tags you chose for each part: when you come back to Part Two, its tags are chosen again.

Right-click a tag in the row (or press **Shift+F10** on it) for **<Ui k="tag-rename-everywhere" />** and **<Ui k="tag-remove-everywhere" />**. Both say how many pages they'll change first. Renaming a tag to one that already exists merges the two.

## Tidy tags

When a project opens, Plot Device looks for tags that seem to be the same one ("Varga famliy" and "Varga family"), and for pages whose tags can't be read. If it finds something new, it says so once and offers **<Ui k="tidy-tags" />**. If not, you won't see anything.

**<Ui k="tidy-tags" />** lists what it found. Merge a pair into whichever spelling you prefer, or open a page to fix it yourself. Nothing changes until you choose, and while something is left to tidy, **<Ui k="tidy-tags" />** stays next to the tags.

## What links here

**<Ui k="linked-from" />** lists every card, page and chapter with a link to this page. Click one to open it. Only links you made count: Plot Device never guesses them from names.

## See a character on the board

Under an outline in **<Ui k="linked-from" />**, **<Ui k="show-on-board" />** opens it as a board with this page's cards outlined in gold and the rest faded. **✕**, or **Esc**, shows every card again.

<Shot name="show-on-board" alt="The board, with the cards about Bea outlined and the others faded" />

## Start an arc

**+ <Ui k="arc-start" />** makes a new outline for this character's story (or this place's, or this idea's), under **<Ui k="story-plot" />**. It starts with a link back to the page, so it shows under **<Ui k="linked-from" />** too.

## Rename

The page's **⋯** has **<Ui k="reference-rename" />** for its name, **<Ui k="reference-rename-file" />** for its file's name, and **<Ui k="versions" />**.

## Other names

A character called "Bea Halmi" may be "Aunt Bea" in the text. Add the other names to the page's file as `aka: [Aunt Bea, Bea]`, and searching for any of them finds the page. See [Character, place and note pages](/plotdevice/files/pages/).
