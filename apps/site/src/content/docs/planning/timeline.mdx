The board shows one outline's plan. The timeline shows **all of them side by side**, one row each, so you can see where one storyline is while another happens: where Bea is while Ilka crosses the river, or what happened in Marrow years before.

Open it with **<Ui k="story-timeline" />**, at the top of **<Ui k="story-plot" />**. It appears once the book has an outline.

<Shot name="timeline" alt="The timeline: the main story on top, with Bea's path and Ilka's arc lined up under its sections" />

## Where the column names come from

The columns are **the sections of your main outline**, the one marked **<Ui k="outline-main-badge" />** in the sidebar and shown first on the timeline. The line above the timeline always says which outline that is.

- **To rename a column**, click its name. That renames the section in the main outline.
- **To add a column**, point between two columns and choose **+**. That adds a section to the main outline.
- **To use another outline's sections as the columns**, choose **<Ui k="timeline-change-main" />** above the timeline, or **<Ui k="outline-make-main" />** in that outline's **⋯**. Nothing in your outlines changes: not the text, not the line-ups. You can switch back at any time.

A new book's first outline is its main outline. The columns are not chapters and not dates: they are the moments of your main plan, in its order. If you want years, make an outline whose sections are years, and make it the main one.

## Line up what happens at the same time

Every other outline is a **storyline**, in a row of its own. A section that happens at the same time as a moment of another storyline can be lined up with it:

- **Drag the section's name** onto that moment: a column's name, or a section in another row. Only the moments that keep every storyline in order light up.
- Or open the section's **⋯** and choose the moment there.

<Shot name="timeline-line-up" alt="A section's menu on the timeline: Unpin, and the moments it can be lined up with" close />

The section keeps its own name and moves into that column. Everything else in its storyline falls into place around it: sections not lined up keep their order, sit between their neighbours and are drawn with a dashed edge, so nothing looks in step when it isn't.

- **<Ui k="timeline-unpin" />**, in the section's **⋯**, undoes a line-up.
- **Chains work:** a storyline lined up with Bea's path, which is lined up with the main story, lines up with the main story too.
- If a line-up stops working, for example because the section it pointed to was deleted, the section says so in words, and its **⋯** offers to line it up again or unpin it.
- If no moment fits, the menu says so. A section that comes before everything the others have can't line up with anything yet.

## Move and add cards

- **Drag a card** to another moment of the same storyline, or use **Move to “…”** in its **⋯**. Cards stay in their own storyline.
- **+ <Ui k="timeline-add-card" />**, under a section, adds a card there.
- A card that links to a chapter shows that chapter as a small tag, so you can see at a glance where each moment is told in the book.

## Hide a row, set an outline aside

- **<Ui k="timeline-hide" />**, in a row's **⋯**, hides that row from the timeline on this computer. The outline doesn't change.
- **<Ui k="outline-set-aside" />** takes an outline out of the way, off the timeline, without deleting anything. See [Set aside or delete an outline](/plotdevice/planning/outlines/#set-aside-or-delete-an-outline).

## What's written in the file

Lining up writes one short line under the section's heading, in the book's language:

```markdown
## Waiting at the landing
At the same time as [Beginning](main-story.md#beginning)

- Meets Ilka off the ferry
```

Unpinning removes that line, leaving the file exactly as it was. Which outline is main is written in `project.md`. See [Outline files](/plotdevice/files/outlines/).
