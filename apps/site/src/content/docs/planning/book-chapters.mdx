**<Ui k="story-manuscript" />**, at the end of the side list in **<Ui k="workspace-story" />**, shows the book as it reads: its parts and chapters, in order.

<Ui k="story-manuscript-hint" />

Click a chapter to open it in **<Ui k="workspace-write" />**.

It's different from an outline. An outline is a plan you shape freely; this is the book itself.
