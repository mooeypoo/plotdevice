A card can point to the chapter it's about and the characters and places in it. Links are always yours to make: Plot Device never guesses them from names.

## Link a card

Choose the card's **⋯ → <Ui k="link-to" />**, then pick a chapter, character, place or note. Type to narrow the list.

<Shot name="link-picker" alt="The link picker: chapters, characters and places to choose from" close />

The link is added at the end of the card's text, as an ordinary Markdown link, so it works in other apps too.

## Follow a link

Click a link in a card: a chapter opens in **<Ui k="workspace-write" />**, and a character, place or note opens as its page.

## Links while you write

When you write a chapter, **<Ui k="shelf-from-outline" />**, on the references shelf, shows every card that links to it, with the characters and places those cards mention, one click away. See [References while you write](/plotdevice/writing/references/).
