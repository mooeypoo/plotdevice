Every book starts with three kinds of pages: characters, places and notes. A book may need another, such as factions in a fantasy novel or ships in a space opera. Add it, and it gets its own place under **<Ui k="story-world" />**, beside the others.

## Add a kind

Choose the book's name at the top, then **<Ui k="kinds-menu" />**. Under **<Ui k="story-world" />**, **+ <Ui k="story-new-kind" />** opens the same window.

Choose **<Ui k="kinds-add" />**, and write what one page is called and what several are called: "Faction" and "Factions". Then **<Ui k="kinds-save" />**.

<Shot name="kinds-dialog" alt="Kinds of pages: Characters, Places, Notes and Factions, each with how many pages it has" />

The new kind shows under **<Ui k="story-world" />**. Its pages work like any other: cards, tags, relationships, links, and the references shelf while you write.

<Shot name="world-own-kind" alt="The Factions gallery under World, with its first page, The Ferrymen" />

## Rename, reorder or remove

Each kind's **⋯** has **<Ui k="kinds-rename" />**, **<Ui k="kinds-move-up" />**, **<Ui k="kinds-move-down" />** and **<Ui k="kinds-remove" />**.

- Renaming changes only what Plot Device calls the kind. Its folder keeps its name (see [Matching folder names](#matching-folder-names)).
- The order is the order under **<Ui k="story-world" />** and on the references shelf.
- Removing a kind deletes nothing. Its pages stay in the book, under **<Ui k="story-unsorted" />**.

## Not sorted yet

A folder of pages that isn't one of the book's kinds shows under **<Ui k="story-unsorted" />**. That happens after removing a kind, or with a folder made in another app. Its pages open, link and show on the shelf as usual. To give it a name, choose **<Ui k="kinds-adopt" />**.

## Matching folder names

After a kind is renamed, its folder keeps the old name. That's fine: nothing needs it to change. If you'd like the folder to match, **<Ui k="kinds-menu" />** offers **<Ui k="kinds-match-folders" />**.

1. It shows each folder, its new name, how many pages it holds, and how many links will be updated.
2. Make a backup first. **<Ui k="match-backup-now" />** copies the whole book to a place you choose. If you already keep one, tick **<Ui k="match-have-backup" />**.
3. **<Ui k="match-rename" />** renames the folders and updates every link to their pages, in chapters, outlines and other pages.

<Shot name="match-folders" alt="Match folder names: the factions folder will become guilds, with its one page; a backup comes first" />

Changed your mind? **<Ui k="folders-undo" />**, in the book's menu, puts every name and link back, until the book is closed.

## What chapters are called

A campaign has sessions; a serial has episodes. Under **<Ui k="kinds-unit-title" />**, write the word for one and for several: "Session" and "Sessions". The chapters list and its buttons then use it. The files and the contents stay as they are.

For how the kinds are kept in the book's files, see [The book's folder](/plotdevice/files/folder/).
