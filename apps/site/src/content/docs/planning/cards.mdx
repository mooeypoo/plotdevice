## Add

- **+ <Ui k="outline-add-card" />**, under a section, adds a card at its end. In the list, **Enter** then starts the next one and **Tab** makes it a detail (see [Outlines](/plotdevice/planning/outlines/)).
- **+ <Ui k="outline-add-section" />**, after the last column, adds a section.
- A card's **⋯ → <Ui k="outline-add-detail" />** adds a detail inside it: a note, a beat, a reminder. Details can have details of their own.

## Edit

Double-click a card, or focus it and press **Enter** or **F2**, to edit its text. Links show as names while you edit; type **@** to add one (see [Linking](/plotdevice/planning/linking/)). Formatting such as `**bold**` works inside cards.

- **<Ui k="item-edit" />**, **<Ui k="outline-add-detail" />** and **<Ui k="link-to" />**.
- **<Ui k="item-move-earlier" />**, **<Ui k="item-move-later" />**, and moving to another section.
- **<Ui k="item-delete" />** asks first, because everything inside the card goes with it. **<Ui k="outline-undo" />** brings it back.

A section's **⋯** renames, moves and deletes the section, and adds a card to it.

## Move

- **Drag** a card. A gold line shows where it will land: over a card's top half it goes before it, over the bottom half after it.
- **With the keyboard:** focus a card and press **Alt+↑** or **Alt+↓** to move it in its section, **Alt+←** or **Alt+→** to move it to the next section (mirrored in a right-to-left interface).
