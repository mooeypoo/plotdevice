An outline is a plan you can rearrange: the main plot, one character's arc, a list of loose ideas. A book can have as many as you like, and none at all is fine. Outlines are under **<Ui k="story-plot" />**, the first half of **<Ui k="workspace-story" />**; characters, places and notes are under **<Ui k="story-world" />** (see [Characters, places and notes](/plotdevice/planning/pages/)).

<Shot name="story" alt="An outline on a board: Beginning, Middle and End" />

## Start an outline

In **<Ui k="workspace-story" />**, choose **+ <Ui k="story-new-outline" />** and give it a title. Click the title at any time to rename it.

## Two ways to see it

- **<Ui k="view-board" />**: each section is a column of cards.
- **<Ui k="view-outline" />**: everything as one indented list, with details folded in and out.

They are two views of the same outline. A change in one is a change in both. The switch is beside the outline's title, because it belongs to the outline.

## Add in the list

<Shot name="story-list" alt="The outline as a list, with + Add card under each section" />

- **+ <Ui k="outline-add-card" />**, under a section, starts a new card at its end. **+ <Ui k="outline-add-section" />** is at the bottom.
- Hover a card (or reach it with the keyboard) for a **+** that adds a detail to it.
- While typing a new line: **Enter** keeps it and starts the next one, **Tab** makes it a detail of the line above, **Shift+Tab** brings it back out, and **Esc** stops.

## Undo, rename and versions

**⋯** beside the views has **<Ui k="outline-undo" />** (also **Ctrl+Z**), **<Ui k="outline-rename-title" />**, and **<Ui k="outline-versions" />**.

## What an outline is, on disk

An ordinary Markdown file in the book's `outlines` folder: `##` headings are sections, list items are cards, and the items under them are details. See [Outline files](/plotdevice/files/outlines/).

An outline is a plan, not the book. Moving a card never moves a chapter.
