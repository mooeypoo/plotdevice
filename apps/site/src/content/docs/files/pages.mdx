---
title: Character, place and note pages
description: How a character, place or note is written, how other names are found, and how relationships are kept.
---

A page is a file in `characters/`, `places/` or `notes/`. The folder says what it is.

```markdown
---
title: Bea Halmi
aka: [Aunt Bea, Bea]
---
Ilka's aunt, who stayed in Marrow when everyone else left.

## Relationships

- Aunt of [Ilka](ilka.md)
- Waits at [the Ferry Landing](../places/ferry-landing.md)

## Habits
Counts the trees aloud. Never sits with her back to the river.
```

- `title` is the page's name. Without one, the first `#` heading names it, then the file name, so pages written in any app work as they are.
- `aka` lists other names; searching for any of them finds the page.
- The first paragraph is the **description**. Everything after it is the **details**.
- Links to other pages are ordinary Markdown links. Plot Device also reads Obsidian's `[[Bea]]` links, but never writes them.

## Relationships

A heading called `Relationships` (or `קשרים`, at any level) holds a list. Each item with a link is one relationship: the words say what it is, the link says with whom. The words can go anywhere: `[Noël](noel.md)'s rival` works too. Items without a link are kept as written, and nested items are details of the item above.

A relationship goes one way. Plot Device shows it on the other page under "Linked from", and never writes it into that page.

Plot Device never rewrites a page to fit. Renaming it changes only its `title`; adding, rewording or removing a relationship changes only that item, and the first one adds the heading after the description.
