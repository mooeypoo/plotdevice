---
title: Report a problem
description: How to tell us when something goes wrong, and what to include.
---

import Ui from '~/components/Ui.astro'

In Plot Device, open **☰** and choose **<Ui k="report-problem" />** or **<Ui k="suggest-idea" />**. Each opens a short form on GitHub; the problem form already has your version and system filled in. You can also start from [GitHub](https://github.com/mooeypoo/plotdevice/issues/new/choose).

For a problem, it helps to include:

- The version: **☰ → About**.
- Your system: Linux (and which one) or Windows.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- A screenshot, if it shows the problem.

Please don't attach your book: a small invented example that shows the problem is just as useful, and your writing stays private.
