Settings belong to you, not to the book: they're kept on your computer, and opening the book elsewhere doesn't carry them along.

## ☰ → <Ui k="settings-open" />

Or **Ctrl+,** (⌘,).

- **<Ui k="settings-language" />**: English or Hebrew, for menus and buttons. Your book keeps its own language.
- **<Ui k="settings-theme" />**: <Ui k="theme-system" />, <Ui k="theme-light" /> or <Ui k="theme-dark" />.
- **<Ui k="settings-check-updates" />**: on at first. Once a day, Plot Device asks GitHub whether a newer version is out, and says so in the top bar. Nothing about you or your writing is sent. See [New versions](/docs/start/install/#new-versions).
- **<Ui k="settings-advanced" />** → **<Ui k="settings-plain-text" />**: off at first. Chapters, pages and outlines in Text open with every Markdown mark showing, for fixing things by hand. See [Plain text](/docs/writing/formatting/#plain-text).
- **<Ui k="settings-advanced" />** → **<Ui k="settings-plain-text-switch" />**: off at first. A **<Ui k="plain-text-switch" />** button beside **<Ui k="toolbar-format" />** switches to plain text and back, as **Ctrl+Shift+M** does.
