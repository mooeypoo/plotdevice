## The top bar

- **The book's title** opens the book menu: **<Ui k="export-book" />**, **<Ui k="kinds-menu" />**, **<Ui k="chapter-versions" />**, and the backups: **<Ui k="backups-menu" />**, **<Ui k="backups-now" />** and **<Ui k="restore-menu" />**. Under the title, it says when the book was last backed up.
- **<Ui k="workspace-story" /> | <Ui k="workspace-write" />** switches between planning and writing. Use them in any order; your place in each is kept.
- **<Ui k="save-saved" />** tells you everything is on disk.
- **Aa** changes how the page looks: text size, page width and focus mode.
- **☰** has the rest: open another book, **<Ui k="settings-open" />**, **<Ui k="help-documentation" />**, and About.

A small **?** beside some places in the app opens the page of this guide about it.

<Shot name="book-menu" alt="The book menu, open under the book's title" close />

## Write

- **The chapter list** shows the book in reading order, with its parts; a part's name folds its chapters. The icon at the start of the row above the page hides and shows it, in Story too (**Ctrl+\\**).
- **The page** is where you write. Select text to format it.
- **<Ui k="shelf" />**, at the end of the row above the page, keeps characters, places and notes beside the page.
- **<Ui k="toolbar-format" />**, at the page's top corner, has every formatting command, for when nothing is selected or you prefer the keyboard.

## Story

**<Ui k="workspace-story" />** is where you plan. Its side list has two halves:

- **<Ui k="story-plot" />** is the story's events: your outlines, with the main one marked **<Ui k="outline-main-badge" />**. **<Ui k="story-timeline" />**, above them, shows them all side by side. **<Ui k="story-manuscript" />**, below them, shows the book as it reads. See [Outlines](/docs/planning/outlines/), [The timeline](/docs/planning/timeline/) and [The book's chapters](/docs/planning/book-chapters/).
- **<Ui k="story-world" />** is who and where: **<Ui k="story-character" />**, **<Ui k="story-place" />** and **<Ui k="story-note" />**, and any [kinds of pages](/docs/planning/kinds/) your book adds. See [Characters, places and notes](/docs/planning/pages/).

<Shot name="story" alt="Story: Plot and World at the side, and the main outline on a board" />

An outline opens on a **<Ui k="view-board" />**, as a **<Ui k="view-outline" />**, or as **<Ui k="view-text" />**, from the switch beside its name.
