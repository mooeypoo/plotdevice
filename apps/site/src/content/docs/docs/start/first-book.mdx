You can start writing within a minute. Planning is there when you want it, never before.

Want to look around first? **<Ui k="welcome-try-sample" />**, on the welcome screen, opens a short invented novel to explore. It's a copy, so change anything you like; **<Ui k="sample-restart" />**, in its book menu, puts it back as it was.

## Start a book

1. On the welcome screen, choose **<Ui k="welcome-new-project" />**. With a book already open, it's in the menu at the top corner, and **<Ui k="prompt-cancel" />**, or Esc, takes you back to the book.
2. Choose the **<Ui k="new-project-language-label" />**. It sets the direction your paragraphs run, Hebrew right to left and English left to right, and the language the template is written in: the cards below show the kinds of pages and outlines you'll get, in that language. You can still mix languages inside the book.
3. Choose what you're making:
   - **<Ui k="template-novel" />:** characters, places and notes, a three-act outline called "Main story", and one for loose ideas.
   - **<Ui k="template-short-story" />:** characters and notes, one chapter ready to write, and a short outline.
   - **<Ui k="template-campaign" />:** NPCs, locations, factions, items and lore, sessions instead of chapters, a session plan and a campaign arc.
   - **<Ui k="template-blank" />:** characters, places and notes, and nothing else.
4. Give the book a **<Ui k="new-project-title-label" />**. The **<Ui k="new-project-author-label" />** is optional.
5. **<Ui k="new-project-location-label" />** starts beside your last book. **<Ui k="new-project-choose" />** picks another place.
6. Choose **<Ui k="new-project-create" />**.

<Shot name="new-project" alt="What are you making? The book's language, four cards: Novel, Short story, D&D campaign and Blank, then the title and where to keep it" />

A template is only a start. Everything in it can be changed afterwards: the [kinds of pages](/docs/planning/kinds/), the outlines, the names. Nothing in the book remembers which template it came from.

## Write

A short story opens in **<Ui k="workspace-write" />** on its chapter. A novel or a campaign opens in **<Ui k="workspace-story" />** on its outline; switch to **<Ui k="workspace-write" />** when you're ready. Choose **+ <Ui k="navigator-new-chapter" />** at the side, give the chapter a title, and start typing.

There is no save button. Plot Device saves a moment after you stop typing, and whenever you switch chapters or leave the window. The top bar says **<Ui k="save-saved" />** when everything is on disk.
