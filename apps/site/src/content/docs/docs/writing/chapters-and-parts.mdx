A book is a list of **chapters**, in reading order. Chapters can be grouped into **parts**, such as "Part One: Arrival". Parts are optional.

## Add a chapter or a part

- **+ <Ui k="navigator-new-chapter" />** adds a chapter right after the one you're writing, and opens it.
- **+ <Ui k="navigator-new-part" />** adds a part at the end of the book. Drag chapters into it.

Chapter numbers are worked out by Plot Device from the reading order. They are never written into your files, so moving a chapter never renumbers anything by hand.

## Rename a chapter

Click the chapter's title above the page and type a new one. **Enter** keeps it and takes you back into the text; **Esc** puts the old title back. Clicking anywhere else keeps it too. A chapter always needs a title, so if you empty it, the old one comes back. Only a poem can be left without a title: see [Writing poems](/docs/writing/poems/#untitled-poems).

## A chapter's menu

Point at a chapter and choose **⋯**:

<Shot name="chapter-menu" alt="A chapter's menu: rename, rename the file, move, and put on the side" close />

- **<Ui k="chapter-rename" />** changes the chapter's title, the same as typing over it above the page.
- **<Ui k="chapter-rename-file" />** changes the name of its file. Renaming the title never renames the file by itself.
- **<Ui k="chapter-move-up" />**, **<Ui k="chapter-move-down" />** and **<Ui k="chapter-move-to" />** move it. See [Moving chapters](/docs/writing/moving-chapters/).
- **<Ui k="chapter-remove" />** takes it out of the reading order without deleting anything. It waits under **<Ui k="navigator-not-in-book" />** until you add it back.

A part's **⋯** renames the part.

## Calling parts something else

A poetry collection has sections rather than parts. To give parts another name, choose the book's title, then **<Ui k="kinds-menu" />**, and under **<Ui k="kinds-part-unit-title" />** write the word for one and for several, such as "Section" and "Sections". The sidebar then says **+ New Section**, and the part's menu uses the word too. Only Plot Device's words change. Chapters can be renamed the same way: see [Kinds of pages](/docs/planning/kinds/#what-chapters-are-called).

## Missing chapters

If a chapter's file was moved or deleted outside Plot Device, it appears under **<Ui k="navigator-missing" />**. When Plot Device finds a likely match, it offers to use it. It never deletes or relinks anything by itself.
