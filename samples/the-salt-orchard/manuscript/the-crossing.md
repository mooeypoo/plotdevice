The ferry left Szeged an hour late. [Ilka](../characters/ilka.md) held the will on her knees, as if it might blow away, though there was no wind at all.

The river was wide and brown and slow. On the far bank a woman in a green coat stood very still at [the landing](../places/ferry-landing.md), one hand raised, not waving.

* * *

"You're thinner than your photograph," said [Aunt Bea](../characters/bea.md), and took the suitcase without asking.

Ilka had prepared a speech on the crossing. She found she had forgotten all of it.
