Before the orchard, there was the river, and before the river there was salt: a white crust on the low fields that no plough could turn and no rain could wash away.

The first Varga to plant there was told he was a fool. He planted anyway. Forty years later the trees still stood, and nobody in [Marrow](../places/marrow.md) could say why.
