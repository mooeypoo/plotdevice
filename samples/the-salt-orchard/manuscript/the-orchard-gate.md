---
title: The Orchard Gate
id: ch-d3m0g3
---
The gate was chained. [Noël](../characters/noel.md) had bolt cutters and no explanation.

"Your grandfather asked me to keep it locked," he said, "until someone came who had a right to open it."

"And you decided that was me."

"The will decided that." He cut the chain. It fell into the grass without a sound, as if [the orchard](../places/salt-orchard.md) had been waiting to swallow it.

* * *

Inside, the rows ran straight to the horizon. Every trunk was ringed with white.
