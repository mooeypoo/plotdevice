id: ch-d3m0l4
---
The ledger was in the kitchen drawer, under the tea towels, where anyone could have found it. That was the first strange thing.

The second was the handwriting. The last entry was dated three weeks after her grandfather's funeral.

> *Picked forty kilos. Sold none. B. says wait for frost.*

Ilka read it twice, then closed the book and went to find Bea.
