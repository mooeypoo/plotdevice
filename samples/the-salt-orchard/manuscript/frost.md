The frost came early that year. Bea said it as if it were a guest she had invited.

"Early for whom?" Ilka asked.

Bea didn't answer. She was already pulling on her boots, and by the time Ilka found her own, the old woman was halfway down the first row with a lantern, touching each trunk as she passed, counting under her breath.
