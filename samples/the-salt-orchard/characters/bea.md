Ilka's aunt, who stayed in Marrow when everyone else left; knows more about the orchard than she will say.

## Habits
Counts the trees aloud. Never sits with her back to the river.

## Secret
She kept the ledger after the funeral. See [Ilka](ilka.md) and the [timeline](../notes/timeline.md).
